What each issue covers

A published figure links to its source. The sample file uses Example source on every figure.

Spend and award volume

By category, agency type, and state, with the quarter compared with the prior quarter and with the same quarter a year earlier.

Growing and declining categories

Which categories are rising and which are falling in the quarter.

Emerging initiatives

Topics the section can hold include AI, cybersecurity, broadband, and modernization of large program systems.

Vendor market share

How the mix of vendors shifts across states.

Price and unit-cost benchmarks

What comparable work costs, and how the unit is scoped, with the source on the figure.

Legislation and model bills

Trends in bills, and model language that shows up in more than one state.

Insights summary

A short reading of the quarter for vendors and for government teams.
