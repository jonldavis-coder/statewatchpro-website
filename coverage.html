How it works & coverage

Arkansas first. Then all 50 states.

We start deep in one state so the data is right, then expand in batches. Here’s how collection works and where rollout stands.

How it works

  1. CollectScheduled, polite collection from public state sources. It follows each site’s robots.txt rules, spaces out requests, and backs off when asked.
  2. Record provenanceEach item keeps its source link and collection date, with activity and error logs.
  3. OrganizeItems are classified by state, agency, service category, and type.
  4. Analyze & alertAI search, dashboards, league tables, insights, email digests, alerts, a quarterly trends report, and a quarterly webinar offered with that report are built on the organized records.

Rollout plan

Step 1 · In progress

Arkansas, in full

Program and policy manuals, agency rules, and the first procurement pages are being collected now, with bids, awards, and legislation loading next.

Step 2

Remaining states

A polite backfill of the remaining states in batches of five, scheduled overnight. Bid portals for all 50 states plus DC are already connected, and news covers every state.

Step 3

Daily monitoring

Ongoing daily monitoring and change alerts across every covered state.

Public data sources

  • Official state procurement and bid portals, award notices, and state contract lists
  • State legislature bill tracking
  • State administrative registers and rules
  • Agency program and policy manuals, policy documents, and transmittals
  • Public news feeds about state procurement
  • FOIA and public-records request guidance, as states publish it. Filing a request on your behalf is a separate service: FOIA filing
