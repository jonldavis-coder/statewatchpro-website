FOIA filing

Your FOIA requests stay private. Any FOIA request you file, and the records you receive, are yours alone. They are never released to the rest of the platform or shared with any other users.

FOIA filing is an optional service at the price on the FOIA filing page: $199 per request, or 1 free request per year for annual members only (not monthly members). You submit the basic request. Staff draft the formal request. You may approve that draft or request changes within 48 hours. If you do not approve it within 48 hours, the request is finalized and sent to the state agency. After it is sent, tracking and status updates are provided to you on each FOIA request.
