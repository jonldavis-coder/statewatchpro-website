FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about coverage, data, and access.

What is StateWatchPro?

State government intelligence for consultants, consulting firms, vendors, and state government teams. It watches state RFPs and solicitations, contract awards, bidders and winners, price benchmarks, legislation, program and policy manual changes, rule changes, and news.

Which states are covered?

Arkansas comes first and is being collected now. Bid portals for all 50 states plus DC are connected and news covers every state; the remaining states are backfilled in batches of five, followed by daily monitoring and change alerts.

Who is it for?

Consultants, consulting firms, and vendors that sell services to state governments, both those pursuing new bids and those protecting and growing contracts they already hold. State government employees use it as well, to compare other states, follow trends, and review what peers pay and how work is scoped.

Can state government employees use StateWatchPro?

Yes. State government teams use it to compare what other states are doing, follow trends across states, see what other states pay and how contracts are scoped, and reuse insights they can adapt. Contact sales to ask about agency licensing.

Is agency licensing different from the listed prices?

Email sales to ask about agency licensing for a government team. The $399 trial and the annual plans stay as published. StateWatchPro is an independent product.

Does it help if we already hold a state contract?

Yes. Watch agency rule, legislation, program and policy manual changes that affect your contracts, track renewal and rebid timing and competitor moves, and find expansion ideas from other states.

Where does the data come from?

Publicly available state government sources. Every record keeps a link to its source and the date it was collected.

How does the AI search work?

You ask questions in plain language across the states you follow. It flags pain points and ideas that carry over between states and links to the documents behind each finding.

Can I filter to just my states?

Yes. Every view can be filtered to one state or any combination of states.

How do I know the data is current?

Collection status and error logs show when each source was last collected and whether anything failed.

How much does it cost?

A 30-day trial is $399 for 1 user and covers every state currently in StateWatchPro; it is credited toward an annual plan bought within 30 days. Annual plans are $3,000 per user per year, $10,000 per year for a 5-user bundle, $25,000 per year for a 10-user bundle, or $35,000 per year for an Enterprise license with up to 30 users. Email digests, alerts, and customizable dashboards are included on every plan. FOIA filing is $150 per request. Annual members get 1 free FOIA request per year. That free request is for annual members only, not monthly members.

What is the FOIA filing service?

To have a FOIA request officially submitted on your behalf costs $150 per FOIA request. The fee covers legal staff who draft, submit, confirm receipt, and track results. The request is sent from our parent company, so your company information is not disclosed. Annual members get 1 free FOIA request per year. That free request is for annual members only, not monthly members, and the 30-day trial does not include it. Use the intake form to send your name, organization, email, state, agency, records sought, date range, preferred format, and notes.

What is the FOIA filing process?

You submit the basic request information. StateWatchPro staff review it and draft the formal FOIA request. Within 48 hours you can approve the draft or request changes. If you do not approve it within 48 hours, the request is finalized and sent to the state agency. After it is sent, tracking and status updates are provided to you on each FOIA request.

Can I get email digests and alerts?

Yes. Saved searches can send an email digest, and you can get an email when a new opportunity matches. Alerts are configurable for new RFPs, amendments, deadlines, awards, renewals, and price or cost changes, and they are delivered in-app and by email.

Can I customize my dashboard?

Yes. Arrange widgets for your states, categories, pipeline, deadlines, trends, and cost comparisons, and save that view for yourself or your team. You can also save a filter set and reopen it.

What is Peer States?

Peer States is for state government employees. It compares spend, prices, vendors, contract terms, and model bills, and benchmarks one state against others. The figures link back to their sources.

What is in the quarterly trends report?

Each quarter, StateWatchPro publishes a State Government Trends Report for the states it covers. An issue covers spend and award volume by category, agency type, and state, quarter over quarter and year over year; growing and declining categories; emerging initiatives such as AI, cybersecurity, broadband, and modernization of large program systems; shifts in vendor market share; price and unit-cost benchmarks; legislation trends and model bills; and a short insights summary. Every figure links to its source. The free preview is a Sample Edition with illustrative data, not a real report.

How do I get the reports?

A free preview downloads the Sample Edition. For the full report, use Get the full report and send your name, organization, role, and whether you are a vendor, a consultant, or with a government team. Quarterly reports, the archive, PDF and CSV downloads, and optional email delivery are included with every plan, including the $399 trial.
