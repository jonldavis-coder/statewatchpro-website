Use cases

For consultants, firms, vendors, and state government teams

Whether you’re chasing a state contract, protecting one you hold, or working in state government, StateWatchPro shows what’s changing across states and what it costs.

Pursuing new bids

For consultants, firms, and vendors looking to win new state work.

Find opportunities early

Track RFPs and solicitations across the states you target, including amendments and deadlines, so you see them while there’s still time to prepare.

Size up competitors

Review who bid and who won by service category, compare vendor capabilities, and see who appears to have the edge.

Set pricing with benchmarks

Use price benchmarks drawn from state documents to understand what states have paid for similar work.

Write stronger responses

Reference RFPs and proposals from other states and address emerging requirements early.

Already holding contracts

For incumbents who want to protect and grow their state contracts.

Watch changes that affect your contracts

Track agency rule, legislation, program and policy manual changes in the states where you deliver, before they surprise your team.

Plan for renewal and rebid

Keep an eye on renewal and rebid timing so you can prepare well before the next solicitation.

Track competitor moves

See which vendors are bidding and winning in your service areas, and where they’re expanding.

Find expansion ideas

Spot programs and good ideas in other states that match what you already deliver, and bring them to new states.

For state government teams

For state government employees who want a practical view of peer states.

Compare what other states are doing

Read solicitations, awards, legislation, and policy changes from other states side by side. Every record links back to its public source.

Follow trends across states

See how bidding, awards, and policy activity move across the states you select, instead of checking one portal at a time.

Cost reporting

See what other states pay and how contracts are scoped. Price benchmarks trace back to the documents they came from.

Insights you can reuse and adapt

Take approaches that show up in other states and adapt them to your own solicitations, policies, and programs.
