Use cases

Built for bid and capture teams

Each role on a pursuit team needs a different slice of state intelligence. StateWatchPro gives each one the view it needs.

By role

Business development

Spot opportunities out for bid early and filter to the states in your territory.

Capture managers

Study past awards, who bid and who won, and the competitive landscape before you commit to a pursuit.

Proposal teams

Pull prior solicitations, Q&A, and award documents from the reference library, and see emerging requirements.

Pricing analysts

Use price benchmarks drawn from state documents to sanity-check what a state has paid for similar work.

Executives

Executive dashboards and standard reports for pipeline reviews and bid/no-bid decisions.

Policy & program watchers

Track legislation, Medicaid and procedure manual changes, and rule changes that shift program requirements.

Questions it helps answer

  • What's out for bid in my states right now?
  • Who has won this kind of work before, and against whom?
  • What did the state pay last time?
  • Which pain points keep showing up across states, and which ideas carried over?
  • Which rule or manual changes will reshape the next solicitation?
