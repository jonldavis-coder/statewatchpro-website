Data & provenance

Every finding traces back to its source

Bid decisions deserve evidence. StateWatchPro is built so you can always see where something came from and how fresh it is.

Our provenance principles

Source on every record

Each solicitation, award, rule, or news item links to the public page or document it came from.

Collection dates

You can see when an item was collected and when its source was last checked.

Visible status & errors

Collection status and error logs show what ran, what changed, and what failed.

AI with citations

AI search answers point to the underlying documents so you can verify before you act.

Public sources

Coverage is built from publicly available state government information.

Human judgment stays in charge

StateWatchPro organizes evidence. Your team makes the bid decision.

What we collect

RFPs & solicitations

Opportunities out for bid, with amendments and deadlines.

Contract awards

What was awarded, to whom, and when.

Who bid, who won

Bidder lists and outcomes where states publish them.

Price benchmarks

What states have paid for comparable work.

Legislation

Medicaid & procedure manuals

Rule changes

Administrative rules as they're proposed and adopted.

News

State-level coverage that gives context to the documents.
