Your FOIA requests stay private.

Any FOIA request you file, and the records you receive, are yours alone. They are never released to the rest of the platform or shared with any other users.

The fee and the free request

$199 per request

To have a FOIA request officially submitted on your behalf costs $199 per FOIA request. The fee covers legal staff who draft, submit, confirm receipt, and track results.

Annual members

1 free FOIA request per year. Annual members only, not monthly. The one-week (7-day) trial does not include this free request. On the intake form, choose the annual member option for that request, or Paid — $199 for any other request.

The FOIA request is sent from our parent company, so your company information is not disclosed. This filing service is separate from the public-records guidance StateWatchPro collects as a data source.

How a request moves

  1. You submit the basicsSend your name, organization, email, state, agency, the records you want, a date range, preferred format, notes, and whether this is an annual member free request or the $199 request.
  2. Staff draft the requestStateWatchPro staff review what you sent and draft the formal FOIA request.
  3. 48 hours for your decisionYou may approve the draft or request changes within 48 hours.
  4. Then it is sentIf you do not approve it within 48 hours, the request is finalized and sent to the state agency.
  5. Tracking and status updatesAfter the request is sent, tracking and status updates are provided to you on each FOIA request.
Your choice during the 48 hours
  • Approve the draft
  • Request changes

Submit the request

Send the basic information on the intake form. It opens your email app. This site does not store the message. If you choose the $199 request and a payment link is configured, Pay $199 uses that link. If no payment link is set, the email is the request.
