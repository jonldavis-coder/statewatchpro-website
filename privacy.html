What this site collects

This marketing website does not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. The report request opens your email app with your name, organization, role, and type. The FOIA intake form opens your email app with your name, organization, email, state, agency, records sought, date range, preferred format, notes, and whether you are using an annual member free request or the $150 request. This site does not store those messages. If you email us, we receive whatever you send. A FOIA request we file is sent from our parent company so your company information is not on the request submitted to the agency.

How we use it

We use the information you send only to reply, arrange a demo, or manage early access. We don't sell personal information.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security and operations.
