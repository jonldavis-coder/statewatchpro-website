Help topics

How do I get help?

Email hello@statewatchpro.com with a short description of what you need. Include the page and state involved if it’s about data.

How do I add or change users?

Email us with the names and number of seats you need. Seats depend on your plan: per user, 5-user, 10-user, or Enterprise (up to 30 users).

Does the trial convert automatically?

No. The $399 trial covers 1 user for 30 days and is credited toward an annual plan bought within 30 days. Email us to choose a plan. The trial does not include the annual-member FOIA request.

How do I request FOIA filing?

Use the FOIA intake form. Annual members get 1 free FOIA request per year (annual only, not monthly). Other requests are $150. Legal staff draft, submit, confirm receipt, and track results. The request is sent from our parent company, so your company information is not disclosed. You may approve the draft or request changes within 48 hours. If you do not approve it within 48 hours, the request is finalized and sent to the state agency. After it is sent, tracking and status updates are provided to you on each FOIA request.

Where does a record come from?

Every item shows its source link and collection date. Open the source to check the original document.

I found an error in the data. What should I do?

Email us using the subject line ‘Data issue’ with the record, the state, the source link, and what looks wrong.
